✦ OUR STORY ✦

Where tradition meets the perfect fit.

✦

RAZA GARMENT is a women’s ready-made blouse boutique in Kandivali West, Mumbai, created for women who love the beauty of traditional Indian craftsmanship with the ease of a ready-to-wear fit.

From rich Kalamkari prints and mirror-work embroidery to sequin borders, contrast piping and carefully finished tie-back necklines, every blouse is selected with an eye for detail, colour and craftsmanship.

We believe a beautiful blouse should not be limited by size. That is why we offer all sizes with expandable fits and tailoring assistance, helping every woman find something that feels made for her.

From our boutique in Mumbai to your wardrobe, RAZA GARMENT brings together colour, craftsmanship and comfort — one blouse at a time.
